The 3 Golden Rules of Japanese Micro-Living

  1. Exploit Vertical Walls: When square footage is scarce, build your storage upwards and onto magnetic surfaces.
  2. Visual Quiet (Danshari): Pick uniform, neutral storage (pure white or matte black) to prevent sensory overload.
  3. Multi-functional Mobility: Opt for items on smooth casters that tuck away out of sight when guests arrive.
